# 切空间
**Definition 1** 切向量与切空间

切向量是光滑流形 $\mathcal{M}$ 上一点 $x_0\in \mathcal{M}$ 上的线性泛函
$$
v:C_{x_0}^{\infty} \to \mathbb{R}
$$

满足线性与Leibuniz律
$$
\begin{dcases}
\forall f,g \in C_{x_0}^\infty ,v(f+g) = v(f)+v(g)\\
\forall f\in C_{x_0}^\infty, \forall \lambda \in \mathbb{R}, v(\lambda f) = \lambda v(f)\\
\forall f,g \in C_{x_0}^\infty, v(f\cdot g) = fv(g)+gv(f)
\end{dcases}
$$
$v$ 称为 $x_0$ 上的导子。
$$
T_{x_0}(M) :=\left\{v:C_{x_0}^{\infty}\to \mathbb{R}\right\}
$$
为 $x_0$ 的切向量张成的线性空间，称为切空间

切向量在流形上的定义是方向导数在流形上的一般化。取$x_0$ 的局部坐标卡 $(x^1,x^2,\cdots, x^n)$

$$
v = \sum_{i = 1}^n v^i\frac{\partial}{\partial x^i}\Big|_{x_0}
$$

$$
v_{x_0}(f) =\sum_{i = 1}^n v^i\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_i}(x_0)
$$


**切向量与坐标转移**
取 $x_0$ 的两个容许坐标卡 $(U,\varphi),(V,\psi)$，切空间的自然基底分别为 $\left\{\dfrac{\partial}{\partial x^i}\right\}$， $\left\{\dfrac{\partial}{\partial y^j}\right\}$

坐标转移
$$
\psi(U\cap V) \overset{\psi^{-1}}{\to} U\cap V \overset{\varphi}{\to} \varphi(U\cap V)
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\partial}{\partial y^i} &= \sum_j \frac{\partial x^j}{\partial y^i}\cdot \frac{\partial}{\partial x^j}\\
&=\sum_j \frac{\partial x^j\circ \psi^{-1}}{\partial y^i}\Big|_{\psi(x_0)} \cdot \frac{\partial}{\partial x^j}\\
&=\sum_j \frac{\partial (\varphi\circ \psi^{-1})^j}{\partial y^i}\Big|_{\psi(x_0)}\cdot\frac{\partial }{\partial x^j}
\end{aligned}
$$

即切空间之间的基变换矩阵表示为Jacobian 矩阵
$$
[v]_y = J^T(x_0)[v]_x
$$


# 切丛

切丛是将流形 $\mathcal{M}$ 的逐点切空间拼接的向量丛，每一个点点切空间构成切丛的fiber
$$
T\mathcal{M} = \bigsqcup_{x\in\mathcal{M}} T_x\mathcal M
$$
从切丛到底流形的投影映射
$$
\pi: T\mathcal M \to \mathcal M, \pi (v_p) = p
$$
